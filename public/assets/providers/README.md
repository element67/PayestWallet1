Official logo files, unmodified, each taken from the provider's own brand or press kit:

- Uniswap: github.com/Uniswap/brand-assets (horizontal logo, pink)
- LI.FI: li.fi/brand-guidelines, "Download brand assets" (horizontal logo, black and white)
- KyberSwap: kyber.network/media-kit (Kyber logo mark, green and white)
- Circle: circle.com/pressroom, Brand Kit, "Download Circle logos" (logo, dark and white)
- AchSwap: the lockup files the AchSwap site publishes (blue and white)

Only a grayscale filter and opacity are applied in the app, for providers that are on hold. Do not redraw or recolor these files.
